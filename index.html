What can I offer?

Throughout my career, I've consistently aligned my design work with measurable business outcomes. I approach each design challenge by first understanding the business metrics we need to move, then crafting solutions that directly address those goals while maintaining an exceptional user experience.

I believe great design starts with asking the right questions. I'm not just looking to execute briefs, but help shape product direction through evidence-based design thinking.

Design succeeds through collaboration. I've led cross-functional teams to deliver successful products, always prioritizing user needs and business goals.

I am proficient in Figma, Adobe Suites, and Jira. I also have experience with React, Node.js, and web development processes.

I respect the process of design. I believe in the power of design to transform lives and businesses. All starts with a heart.
